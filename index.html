How reviews work

Recall before revealing. Reviews use calendar days: Forgot → tomorrow; Unsure → 2 days; Remembered → 3 days initially, then 6 days and progressively longer intervals (up to 365 days). Forgot resets the interval and reduces future growth; Unsure brings the word back in 2 days. This is a day-based SM-2 adaptation, not Bubei’s proprietary algorithm.

Overdue learning steps come first, followed by overdue reviews. A new group is available each day while unstudied words remain. Unfinished groups resume on the same day. You can continue with additional groups after finishing. Due reviews remain available separately.
